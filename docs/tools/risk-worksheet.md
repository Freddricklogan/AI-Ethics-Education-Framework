# AI Impact Assessment — risk worksheet

Section 5.2 of the [AI Impact Assessment template](../ai-impact-assessment.md) asks for a likelihood and an impact (High / Medium / Low) and a mitigation for each of six risks; Section 7 asks the committee for one of five recommendations. Section 1.2 of the [deployment guidelines](../responsible-ai-deployment.md) lists fourteen vendor red flags. This page collects all three and turns them into a recommendation you can paste into the assessment.

**What is the framework's and what is the worksheet's.** The six risks, the H/M/L scales, the red flags and the five recommendations are the framework's. The framework does not say how likelihood and impact combine, so the worksheet uses a 3×3 matrix — High × High is *Critical*, High × Medium or Medium × High is *High*, Medium × Medium and High × Low are *Medium*, the rest *Low* — and the following ladder, which your committee should read before relying on it:

| Condition | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Any risk not yet rated | Defer |
| A Critical risk with no mitigation written | Deny |
| A Critical risk with a mitigation, or a High risk with none | Pilot required |
| A High risk with a mitigation, or any vendor red flag | Approve with conditions |
| Otherwise | Approve |

Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
