# AI Impact Assessment Template

## Instructions

Complete this assessment before deploying any AI tool in an educational setting. This assessment should be completed collaboratively by the requesting educator or administrator, the IT department, and at least one member of the AI governance committee. All sections must be completed before the tool can be approved for use.
