# AI Ethics in Education Framework

A comprehensive framework providing guidelines, assessment tools, and curriculum resources for the ethical development, deployment, and governance of artificial intelligence in educational settings.

## Overview

As AI technologies become increasingly integrated into education -- from intelligent tutoring systems and automated grading to predictive analytics and personalized learning platforms -- the need for robust ethical guidelines has never been more critical. This framework addresses the unique ethical challenges that arise when AI systems are used in educational contexts, where the stakes involve learners' futures, privacy, and equitable access to opportunity.

## Framework Components

### Core Documents

- **Ethical Principles** (`docs/ethical-principles.md`) - The foundational principles guiding ethical AI use in education: fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and inclusivity
- **Bias Assessment Framework** (`docs/bias-assessment-framework.md`) - Systematic methods for identifying, measuring, and mitigating bias in educational AI systems
- **Responsible AI Deployment** (`docs/responsible-ai-deployment.md`) - Guidelines for piloting, deploying, monitoring, and governing AI tools in educational institutions
- **AI Literacy Curriculum** (`docs/ai-literacy-curriculum.md`) - A comprehensive curriculum for teaching AI literacy to students and educators

### Templates

- **AI Impact Assessment** (`templates/ai-impact-assessment.md`) - A structured template for evaluating AI tools before deployment in educational settings

## Who This Is For

- **School and district administrators** making decisions about AI tool adoption
- **Teachers and instructional designers** integrating AI into learning experiences
- **EdTech developers** building AI-powered educational products
- **Policy makers** drafting regulations for AI in education
- **Researchers** studying the impact of AI on learning outcomes and equity
- **Students and parents** seeking to understand how AI affects education

## Key Principles at a Glance

1. **Fairness** - AI systems must not discriminate against students based on race, gender, socioeconomic status, disability, or other protected characteristics
2. **Transparency** - Students, parents, and educators must understand how AI systems make decisions that affect learning
3. **Accountability** - Clear lines of responsibility must exist for AI-driven decisions in education
4. **Privacy** - Student data must be protected with the highest standards of privacy and security
5. **Inclusivity** - AI in education must be designed to serve all learners, including those with disabilities and those from underrepresented communities

## Getting Started

1. Read the **Ethical Principles** document to understand the foundational values
2. Use the **AI Impact Assessment** template before adopting any new AI tool
3. Apply the **Bias Assessment Framework** to evaluate existing AI systems
4. Follow the **Responsible AI Deployment** guidelines for implementation
5. Incorporate the **AI Literacy Curriculum** into your educational programs
